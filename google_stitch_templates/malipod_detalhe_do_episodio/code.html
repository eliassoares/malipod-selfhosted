Show Notes & Description

In this landmark episode, we dive deep into the Malipod ecosystem and how Podcasting 2.0 tags are revolutionizing the way we experience audio. We explore the technical hurdles of cross-device synchronization and the philosophy behind an open, decentralized podcasting future.

Key topics covered:

  • The evolution of RSS namespaces for modern creators.
  • Real-time transcript synchronization via WebSockets.
  • How chapter markers can improve accessibility and listener engagement.
  • A look behind the scenes at the Malipod design system.

Special guest Dr. Elena Vance joins us to discuss the impact of decentralized protocols on digital sovereignty.
